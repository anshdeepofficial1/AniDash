Installation Guide

Safe, private, and simple.

1. Download the APK

Choose arm64-v8a for modern smartphones or Universal for all devices. Chrome or your browser may show a "File might be harmful" prompt — this is standard for all side-loaded open-source APKs.

2. Allow Unknown Sources

When opening the downloaded APK, tap Settings if prompted and toggle on "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager.

3. In-Place Updates

When installing a newer version over an older one, your watch history, bookmarked anime, downloaded episodes, and custom themes are 100% preserved!
