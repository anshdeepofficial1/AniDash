Legal

Content & licensing.

AniDash is a client application, not a media hosting service.

Important information

Content

AniDash does not host anime or manga files. External content may have separate rights and terms. Only access or download content you are authorized to use.

Third-party services

Metadata, account integrations and playback availability depend on third-party services. They can change, fail, or remove content.

Privacy

Guest libraries and progress may be stored locally. Optional account integrations follow the respective provider’s privacy and authorization terms. Avoid entering credentials outside the official authorization screen.

Open-source license

The native AniDash project is available under Apache License 2.0. Third-party code and media retain their respective licenses.
