Full Feature Guide

Everything built for anime fans.

AniDash unites multi-audio streaming (SUB, DUB & Hindi), a zero-stall video player, an intelligent offline downloader, AniList sync, and a full manga reader in one seamless dark interface.

Audio & Languages

1. Stream in SUB, DUB or Hindi (हिन्दी डब)

Watch anime in your preferred language without having to switch between different apps or websites.

🇯🇵

Japanese SUB (Original Audio)

Experience the passion of original Japanese voice actors with perfectly synchronized subtitles in English, Spanish, and multiple languages.

🇺🇸

English DUB Catalog

Enjoy massive libraries of English dubbed anime with professional voice casts. Perfect for multitasking or action-packed battle sequences.

🇮🇳

Hindi Dubbed Anime (हिन्दी डब)

Specially curated for Indian fans! Stream and download popular series like Naruto, Dragon Ball Super, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, and Solo Leveling in Hindi audio.

🎛️

1-Tap Audio Switcher

Switch between SUB, DUB, and HINDI audio tracks in seconds right inside the player controls or from the episode details menu.

🧠

Smart Language Memory

Set your default language preference once, and AniDash automatically plays every subsequent episode in your favorite audio track whenever available.

⚙️

Custom Subtitle Tuning

Fine-tune subtitle delay by milliseconds (+/-), adjust font sizes, change text shadow, background opacity, and font styling for maximum readability.

Playback Engine

2. Zero-Stall Streaming & Video Player

Powered by an optimized ExoPlayer engine with intelligent auto-recovery that eliminates buffer freezing.

⚡

Smart Buffer Watchdog

Real-time playback health monitor detects network stalls, micro-nudges the stream at 4s, and soft-recovers at 7s without user interruption.

🛡️

Playlist Cache Bypass

Ensures HLS .m3u8 manifest files are always fetched fresh from upstream, eliminating the bug where streams froze after ~100s.

👆

Intuitive Touch Gestures

Swipe up/down on the left side for brightness, on the right side for volume, and double-tap left/right for instantaneous 10s seek.

📐

Precision 16:9 & Notch Alignment

Edge-to-edge immersive portrait view with no empty top padding, plus safe-area notch margins in fullscreen landscape mode.

🖼️

Picture-in-Picture (PiP)

Seamlessly minimize video into a floating window so you can browse other apps or chat while keeping your anime playing.

⏭️

Skip Intro & Outro

Convenient one-tap buttons to jump over opening and ending themes so you dive straight into the story.

Offline Viewing

3. Intelligent Offline Downloader

Download full anime episodes for flights, road trips, and offline watching with live controls.

📥

Direct Episode Download Rings

Interactive progress rings on every episode card. Tap to pause, resume, or cancel downloads directly without jumping to the download tab.

📱

Cellular Data Protection

Optimized 6-worker HLS segment downloading eliminates CDN socket timeouts and aggressive retry data consumption on 4G networks.

📂

Custom Storage Paths

Choose between internal app sandbox or public phone storage (Download/Movies) to easily access videos in VLC or external apps.

📦

Batch Download Controls

Select multiple episodes in the Episodes tab and pause, resume, or cancel the whole batch with a single tap.

🔄

Background Download Queue

Queues episodes reliably in the background with persistent foreground notification service so downloads never get killed by Android.

✓

Seamless Resume

Play offline downloaded episodes seamlessly with full resume timestamp tracking synchronized with your online watch history.

Ecosystem & Tracking

4. Extensions, MangaDex & Cloud Sync

Uncapped source extensions, integrated manga reading, and two-way AniList/MAL synchronization.

🌐

Multi-Source Extensions

Built-in scrapers for Hianime, Jdudu, Hindi providers, Consumet, and AnimePahe. If one source is slow, switch in seconds without losing progress.

📚

Integrated MangaDex Reader

Read 50,000+ manga titles with continuous vertical webtoon scroll, chapter prefetching, reading history, and offline saving.

▤

AniList & MAL Cloud Sync

Two-way cloud synchronization for Watching, Completed, Planning, Paused and Dropped lists with automatic episode progress tracking.

🎨

Pure Black AMOLED Themes

Light, Dark, and Pure Black AMOLED modes with a full custom hex color palette picker to customize the app's visual identity.

▦

5 Episode Layout Styles

Switch between Compact List, Rich Banner, Dense Grid, Minimalist Block, and Classic List views for episodes and library items.

🛡️

100% Ad-Free & Private

Zero advertisements, zero tracking, zero telemetry, and no account required to watch. Completely open-source under Apache 2.0.
