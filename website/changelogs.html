v3.3.0
Latest Release
Current Stable

Official JustAnime Default & Video Freeze Fix 🚀

  • Official JustAnime Default: Resolved an issue where initial app launch defaulted to third-party extension sources. The app now cleanly opens on official native JustAnime by default while retaining user-selected extensions.
  • Resolved Video Freeze / Audio Desync: Fixed decoder lockup where video would freeze while audio continued playing. Removed non-IDR access point overrides and isolated upstream DataSource connections.
  • MediaCodec Hardware Fallback: Enabled automatic decoder fallback and live dropped-frame recovery to resync video output on hardware glitches.

v3.2.7
Earlier

Zero-Stall Streaming, Multi-Audio & Watchdog Recovery 🚀

  • Playlist Cache Bypass: Built custom FilterPlaylistCacheDataSource so HLS .m3u8 manifests bypass disk caching and always fetch live, eliminating the ~100s buffer halt bug.
  • Playback Health Watchdog: Added background watchdog that detects buffer stalls, micro-nudges at 4s, and soft-recovers at 7s without user interruption.
  • Multi-Audio (SUB, DUB & Hindi) Experience: Enhanced audio switching and stream metadata indicators.
  • Extractor & Socket Hardening: Enabled Access Unit Delimiter and non-IDR keyframe extraction for MPEG-TS streams to prevent demuxer lockups.

v3.2.6
Oct 2026

Zero-Stall Streaming & Direct Download Controls 🚀

  • Zero-Stall Stream Watchdog: Implemented active buffer recovery and bypassed playlist caching in ExoPlayer to eliminate HLS playback freezes.
  • Interactive Episode Download Rings: Tap downloading episodes to Pause, tap paused episodes to Resume, and tap queued episodes to Cancel directly on episode cards.
  • Batch Multi-Select Controls: Pause, Resume, or Cancel multiple selected downloads in the Episodes tab with one tap.
  • Cellular Data Protection: Tuned HLS download concurrency down to 6 workers to eliminate CDN socket timeouts and retry loops on 4G networks.
  • UI Overlap Fix: Fixed details screen cover banner drop-down overlap and made the "Watch Now" FAB collapse automatically on downward scroll.
