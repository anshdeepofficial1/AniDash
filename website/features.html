Features

All the ways to use AniDash.

A closer look at the native app and the browser companion.

Discover, organize and read.

01 / 04

Find your next anime

Browse trending, popular, upcoming and recently updated titles. Search series and films, then check ratings, synopsis, episode information and available languages.

02 / 04

Make it your watchlist

Keep Watching, Completed, Planning, Paused and Dropped lists. Resume supported episodes from saved progress. Connect AniList for compatible tracking and sync.

03 / 04

Read manga

Browse supported manga in the native app through MangaDex. The browser app has catalogue discovery, publisher links where available and a local CBZ reader.

04 / 04

Take episodes offline

Manage downloads of content you are authorized to save in the native app. Saved episodes can be watched without internet; browser playback requires a connection.

Playback & personalization.

Capabilities vary by platform and content availability.

Language preferences

Japanese audio with subtitles and English dubs when available. Additional language tracks, including Hindi, depend on content and source.

Player controls

Supported players offer playback controls, quality and subtitle options, and saved progress. Exact controls vary by platform.

Filler & watch order

Episode details and supported filler markers help you follow a series.

Themes & layouts

Customize native app colors, dark and light themes, card styles and episode layouts.

Tracking

Optional AniList integration, local lists and web tracking integrations where configured.

Notifications

Native controls for release, update, Continue Watching and download notifications.

AniDash does not promise uninterrupted playback, complete catalogues or universal language availability. Use media you have permission to access.
