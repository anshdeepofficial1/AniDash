About

One place for your anime routine.

AniDash is an open-source anime companion focused on a clean, connected experience across discovery, playback, progress, downloads and manga—now available on Android, Windows and macOS.

Why AniDash exists

The goal is to reduce the number of separate tools needed to discover a title, understand it, watch it, remember progress and manage a personal library.

Mobile and desktop

Android remains available as a universal APK, while Windows and macOS now have native desktop builds with platform-specific packaging and desktop navigation.

Download AniDashSee platforms

Desktop-first details

The Windows edition includes native window controls, Setup and portable distribution options. The macOS edition is distributed as a DMG and uses the desktop-oriented AniDash layout.

Open source

The source is public, issues can be reported on GitHub, and focused contributions can be reviewed through the project repository.
