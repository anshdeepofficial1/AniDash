Features

More than just a player.

AniDash unites lightning-fast streaming, an intelligent offline downloader, AniList sync, and a full manga reader in one seamless dark interface.

⚡

Zero-Stall Playback

Smart buffer watchdog detects stalled network segments and auto-recovers the stream in the background without needing to exit the player.

📥

Direct Episode Downloads

Interactive progress rings on every episode card. Tap to pause, resume, or cancel downloads directly without jumping to the download screen.

📱

Cellular Data Protection

Optimized 6-worker HLS segment downloading eliminates CDN socket drops, timeouts, and aggressive retry data consumption on 4G networks.

📂

Custom Storage Paths

Choose between internal app sandbox or public phone storage (Download/Movies) to easily access videos in external players.

🔊

SUB / DUB Preference

Set your default language preference once, and AniDash will automatically pick English Dub or Original Japanese Sub whenever available.

✓

Continue Watching

Instant resume from the exact second you stopped, syncing timestamps across online streams and downloaded offline files.

⌕

Discovery & Smart Search

Explore trending anime, seasonal lineups, top-rated series, and search with instant filters for genres, status, and sub/dub audio.

▤

AniList & MAL Sync

Two-way cloud synchronization for Watching, Completed, Planning, Paused and Dropped anime lists with episode progress tracking.

📚

Integrated Manga Reader

Browse and read thousands of manga through MangaDex with page prefetching, continuous vertical scroll, chapter history, and offline saving.

🎨

Material You & AMOLED

Light, Dark, Pure Black AMOLED, and full custom hex color palette generator to customize the app's visual identity to your style.

▦

5 Layout Display Styles

Switch between Compact List, Rich Banner, Dense Grid, Minimalist Block, and Classic List views for episodes and library cards.

🌐

Multiple Source Extensions

Built-in support for top sources like Jdudu, Hindi streams, visuals, and raw sources for maximum availability and server reliability.
